Milestone 1Week 1

Foundation and design system

Brand fonts, color palette, and global styles set up in Squarespace, the eight-item navigation, and the live home hero and header shown in the Design Proof tab. You end the week with a styled, on-brand foundation live in your own site.

Milestone 2Week 2

Home page build

Inner page templates

Reusable templates for Case Studies (category and detail), Services (Merchandise, Web Services, Activations), B-Corp and Impact, News, and a Contact page with proper lead capture. Your team duplicates these and publishes, no developer needed.

SEO, analytics, performance, and handover
